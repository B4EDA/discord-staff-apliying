## Evaluating Applications

### Key Information to Review

When viewing an application, focus on:

#### 1. **Server Level (1-50 scale)**
- **1-10**: New members - may need more server experience
- **11-25**: Regular contributors - good foundation
- **26-40**: Trusted members - preferred candidates
- **41-50**: Legends - ideal for leadership roles

**What to consider:**
- Higher level doesn't guarantee capability
- A level 15 with great scenario response might be better than level 40 with poor response
- Look for consistency in their answers

#### 2. **Moderation Scenario Response**
This is the most important section! Evaluate based on:

##### ✅ **Good Responses Show:**
- Clear step-by-step approach
- De-escalation attempt first
- Fair and impartial treatment
- Community safety prioritized
- Professional tone
- Acknowledgment of context vs. severity
- Documentation/logging of action

**Example of a good response:**
> "I would first DM the member privately to understand their intention without calling them out publicly. If genuinely joking, I'd remind them of the rules and the impact. If spam continues, I'd issue a warning. If they persist, I'd mute them temporarily and document the incident. I'd also inform management for pattern tracking."

##### ❌ **Red Flags to Watch:**
- Immediate ban/harsh punishment
- Emotional or personal language
- Ignoring context entirely
- No de-escalation attempt
- Inconsistent with server rules
- Revenge/anger-driven decisions
- No follow-up or documentation
- Dismissive of other members' concerns

**Example of a poor response:**
> "Ban them immediately. They're just being toxic."

##### 🤔 **Average Responses:**
- Shows understanding but lacks depth
- One-dimensional approach
- Limited follow-up thinking
- Doesn't address all aspects

## Making Decisions

### Decision Framework

Use this to decide Accept/Reject:

#### ✅ **Accept Candidate If:**
- Level 15+ (or exceptional level 10-14 with excellent scenario response)
- Scenario response shows maturity and fairness
- Time commitment aligns with needs
- Professional communication in all answers
- Willing to follow rules and learn

#### ⏸️ **Request Interview If:**
- Great scenario response but lower server level
- Inconsistent but promising answers
- Time commitment seems tight but flexible
- Want to assess communication skills in person

#### ❌ **Reject Candidate If:**
- Level too low (under 10) AND poor scenario response
- Scenario shows bias, anger, or rule violation
- Unavailable enough hours
- Poor communication quality
- Doesn't agree with server values
- Red flags in background questions

## Common Scenario Analysis

### The Scenario:
> "Member starts sending spam messages and mentions controversial topic causing conflict. They claim it's just joking. Other members are upset and reporting."

### What to Look For:

#### ✅ **Strong Indicators:**
- "I'd message them first to understand"
- "Check if this fits the server rules"
- "Consider if it's isolated or pattern"
- "Balance member's intent with impact"
- "Document and inform leadership"
- "Fair warning before escalation"

#### ⚠️ **Warning Signs:**
- "Ban immediately"
- "These people are always trouble"
- "I'd just delete and ignore"
- "Hit them with punishment"
- "Controversial topics? Delete everything"

#### 🎯 **Ideal Response Includes:**
1. **Information Gathering** - Assess situation fully
2. **Communication** - Talk to member first
3. **Rule Application** - Check what rules apply
4. **Proportional Response** - Match punishment to offense
5. **Documentation** - Log the incident
6. **Team Notification** - Inform leadership
7. **Follow-up** - Monitor for patterns

## Special Situations

### When to Interview Instead of Immediate Accept/Reject

**Consider an interview when:**
- Great potential but lower level
- Different communication style (not bad, just different)
- Excellent scenario response despite weak background
- Unclear answers that need clarification
- They seem overqualified (verify genuine interest)
- Boundary cases you're unsure about

**Interview Questions:**
1. Why specifically our server? (vs generic answer)
2. Walk me through a conflict you resolved
3. What would you do if staff disagreed with your moderation?
4. How do you handle emotional situations?
5. What's your experience with systems like Discord?

### When to Reject Clearly

**No interview needed if:**
- Clear red flags in scenario (unfair, vindictive, ignorant)
- Server level 5 or below (unless exceptional)
- Won't be available at all
- Poor communication across all answers
- Violates core server values

## Admin Panel Features Explained

### Filter Buttons
- **All**: See every application
- **New**: Only unreviewed (start here!)
- **Accepted**: Your approved team members
- **Rejected**: Archived rejections

### Table Columns
- **Discord User**: Their username (verify they're real server member)
- **Level**: Server level (quick reference)
- **Role Applied**: Mod or Staff
- **Status**: Current state
- **Date**: When they applied
- **Actions**: View/Delete buttons

### View Button
Shows complete application with:
- All personal info
- Full scenario response (read carefully!)
- Time commitment and motivation
- Accept/Reject buttons at bottom

### Delete Button
Permanently removes application (can't undo!)
- Use for spam/fake applications
- Or after processing if you want to clean up

### Clear All Data
⚠️ **DANGEROUS** - Deletes every application!
- Only use to reset for testing
- Or starting completely fresh
- Ask team before using!

## Best Practices

### ✅ Do:
- Review applications within 48 hours
- Read scenario responses carefully
- Look for growth potential, not just experience
- Consider team balance and diversity
- Take notes on each application
- Involve multiple admins in decisions
- Give clear feedback if interviewed
- Archive decisions for future reference
- Be consistent in evaluation criteria

### ❌ Don't:
- Make snap judgments based on level alone
- Bias toward people you know
- Reject for "edgy" humor in scenario
- Skip the scenario response
- Make decisions while angry/emotional
- Ignore red flags for friends
- Use it as punishment system
- Forget to follow up with candidates
- Change criteria mid-selection

## Troubleshooting Admin Panel

### Applications not showing?
- They were submitted from a different browser
- Check browser localStorage settings
- Try the filter buttons
- Refresh the page (F5)

### Can't change status?
- Click the application first (View button)
- Status buttons are in the modal
- Close modal and reopen to verify

### Data disappeared?
- Check browser cache wasn't cleared
- Verify you're in Admin panel
- Try refreshing the page
- Data is stored per-browser

## Questions for Applicant Follow-up

If you want to ask more, consider:

1. What server rules are most important to you?
2. How would you handle a moderator being wrong?
3. What's your experience with [specific Discord features]?
4. How do you balance strictness with friendliness?
5. What would make you step down as staff?
6. How do you handle burnout?
7. Tell us about your best and worst moderation experience?
